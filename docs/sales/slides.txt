## Slide 1

# Indian HVAC Supply Chains Are Entering a New Operating Reality

### Headline

Demand volatility, rising service expectations, and fragmented warehouse operations are creating a growing profitability gap across HVAC manufacturers.

### Key observations

* Cooling demand increasingly concentrated into short seasonal windows
* Channel partners expect near-real-time fulfillment
* Spare parts availability directly impacts AMC performance
* Working capital pressure increasing across inventory-heavy businesses
* Existing ERP/WMS investments still leave significant operational work outside the system

### Executive takeaway

The next competitive advantage is no longer planning.

It is execution speed.
