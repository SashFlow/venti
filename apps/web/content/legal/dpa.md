# Data Processing Agreement (DPA)

This Data Processing Agreement ("DPA") forms part of our Terms of Service between you ("Customer") and us ("Provider").

## 1. Scope and Applicability
This DPA applies where and only to the extent that we process Personal Data on your behalf in the course of providing the Service and such Personal Data is subject to Data Protection Laws (such as GDPR, CCPA, or HIPAA).

## 2. Roles and Responsibilities
- **Customer as Controller**: You are the controller or business owner of the Personal Data.
- **Provider as Processor**: We are the processor or service provider.

## 3. Security of Processing
We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to ensure a level of security appropriate to the risk, including encryption of data at rest and in transit, and role-based access controls.

## 4. Sub-processors
Customer provides general authorization for us to engage Sub-processors as listed in our [Sub-processor List](./sub-processors.md).

## 5. Data Subject Rights
We will assist you in responding to requests from individuals exercising their rights under Data Protection Laws (e.g., right to access, delete, or port data).

## 6. Data Transfers
If Personal Data is transferred outside the EEA/UK, we will ensure that such transfers are subject to appropriate safeguards (e.g., Standard Contractual Clauses).
